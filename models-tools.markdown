# Models & Tools

Our research develops sophisticated forecasting systems that combine statistical rigor with mechanistic understanding of disease ecology. We create tools that translate complex scientific models into practical decision support systems for public health agencies.

## Disease Risk Modeling Approaches

<img src="/assets/images/statistical-mechanistic.png" alt="Disease spillover risk diagram" class="float-right">

### Statistical Models
Fitted to human case data for spatiotemporal models and risk mapping. Often generalize poorly

### Hybrid Models
Combine statistical fitting with mechanistic understanding, incorporating key ecological processes e.g., host abundance dynamics

### Mechanistic Models
Based on mathematical description of key biological parameters. Difficult to calibrate
